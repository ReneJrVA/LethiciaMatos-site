FAQ

How do you photograph weddings?

I love to stand by your side to help in the run-up to the big day. You will receive a guide from me with tips for the day's schedule, the best moments for the couple photos, and advice on lighting... And I absolutely love receiving photos of the dress, shoes, and invitations in the meantime! On the wedding day itself, for me, it is all about capturing the feeling, the atmosphere, and the emotions. From the quiet to the crazy moments, and all the details that make your wedding unique. That is why I consciously choose to photograph non-traditional and relaxed weddings.

Where are you based, and do you travel?

I am based in Antwerp (Belgium), and yes: I love to travel! Would you like me to come and capture your story on location? In that case, I will charge a travel fee, depending on the destination. But don't worry: I always look for the most affordable way to get there. Discovering new places gives my creativity a huge boost, and it is what I love doing most.

When will we receive the photos?

You will receive a link to an online gallery with all your photos within a maximum of six weeks (depending on the season, this can sometimes be faster). Through this gallery, you can download all the images, share them, and pick out your favorites!

How many photos can we expect?

That depends on the package you choose. I will provide the exact number of photos when you contact me via email.
